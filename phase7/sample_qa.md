# Sample Q&A - ICICI Prudential MF Facts Assistant

## Factual Queries

### Q1: What is the expense ratio of ICICI Prudential Large Cap Fund Direct Growth?

**Answer:**  
The Total Expense Ratio (TER) for ICICI Prudential Large Cap Fund Direct Growth is 0.42% as of March 2024. This is significantly lower than the regular plan due to the absence of distribution commissions.  
Source: https://www.icicipruamc.com/downloads/factsheet/large-cap-fund-mar2024.pdf  
Last updated: March 31, 2024

---

### Q2: What is the exit load for ICICI Prudential Multi Asset Fund?

**Answer:**  
The exit load is 1% if redeemed within 1 year from the date of allotment. No exit load is charged for redemptions after 1 year. This applies to both SIP and lumpsum investments.  
Source: https://www.icicipruamc.com/downloads/kim/multi-asset-fund.pdf  
Last updated: March 31, 2024

---

### Q3: What is the minimum SIP amount for ICICI Prudential Nifty Next 50 Index Fund?

**Answer:**  
The minimum SIP amount for ICICI Prudential Nifty Next 50 Index Fund Direct Growth is ₹5,000. Additional investments can be made in multiples of ₹1.  
Source: https://groww.in/mutual-funds/icici-prudential-nifty-next-50-index-fund-direct-growth  
Last updated: April 19, 2024

---

### Q4: What is the benchmark of ICICI Prudential Large & Mid Cap Fund?

**Answer:**  
The benchmark for ICICI Prudential Large & Mid Cap Fund Direct Plan Growth is NIFTY LargeMidcap 250 TRI (Total Return Index). This index tracks the performance of the top 250 companies by market capitalization in India.  
Source: https://www.icicipruamc.com/downloads/factsheet/top-100-fund-mar2024.pdf  
Last updated: March 31, 2024

---

### Q5: How do I download my capital gains statement from ICICI Prudential?

**Answer:**  
To download your capital gains statement from ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund:  
1. Visit the ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund website (www.icicipruamc.com)  
2. Login to your investor account using PAN and password  
3. Go to 'Transaction History' or 'Statements' section  
4. Select 'Capital Gains Statement'  
5. Choose the financial year and download the PDF  
Source: https://www.icicipruamc.com/investor-services  
Last updated: March 31, 2024
